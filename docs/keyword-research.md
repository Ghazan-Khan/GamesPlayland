# Keyword research — GamesPlayLand

## Method and limits

This research is deliberately qualitative. **No search-volume, difficulty, CPC or traffic numbers are reported**, because none were verified with a keyword tool. Any number here is a count of *pages we built* or *levels we shipped*, not an estimate of demand.

What *was* verified:

- The live search results for the head terms and several long-tail terms (checked 18 November 2026). The pages and publishers cited below are real and currently rank for those queries.
- The authoritative facts about the original *Parking Fury*: developer Andriy Pidvirnyy, released 26 May 2016, 10 levels, WASD/arrow-key driving, browser + mobile. These come from the developer-hosted and large-portal pages listed below.
- The shape of the competitive set: large portals, aggregators and game hubs dominate the head terms; few of them publish genuinely original, in-depth editorial for the long tail.

Competition labels (High / Medium / Low) are **judgements from the observed SERP**, not tool-derived KD scores. They describe how hard the term looked on the day we checked, and will change.

**Reading of the SERP:** the head terms are occupied by well-established portals with strong domain signals and thousands of user votes. Competing on the exact head term alone is unrealistic for a new domain. The opening is in (1) genuinely playable original games, (2) honest, specific long-tail help — controls, levels, walkthroughs — and (3) mobile/touch support, which several large competitors treat as a footnote.

## Positioning

- We are **not** the 2016 *Parking Fury* and do not claim to be. Every game page carries a plain disclaimer. Our games are original implementations with our own physics, levels and artwork.
- We do **not** publish player counts, star ratings from users, or “played X times” figures. Those are unverifiable on a new site.
- We compete on specificity: exact level counts, exact control schemes, real par times and levels that a build-time solver has proved solvable.

## Content-to-intent mapping

Every mapped URL has one job and one primary term family. We deliberately did **not** build separate thin pages for tiny variants such as “parking fury unblocked” or “parking fury online free”; those queries are answered inside the relevant game page's copy and FAQ. This avoids doorway pages and keeps link equity consolidated.

See `keyword-map.csv` for the row-by-row map used during the build.

## What we will measure (not what we claim)

Rankings, impressions and clicks will be read from Search Console once the site is verified and has enough data. Until then there is no traffic claim on this site or in these docs.
