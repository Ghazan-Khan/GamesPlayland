About Parking Jam

The idea is simple. Every car in the lot can only move along its own axis — horizontal cars slide left and right, vertical cars slide up and down. Your job is to clear a path from the red car to the exit on the right-hand side of the third row.

What makes it a puzzle rather than a chore is the order. A car that looks like the obvious blocker is often not the real one, and moving the wrong vehicle first can trap you in a state that needs a reset. Every puzzle can be solved, and the par score tells you how many single-space slides a tidy solution takes.

There is no timer, so you can sit and think. Use the undo button when you want to test an idea without committing, and use the reset button when a tangle has gone too far.

How to play

  1. Press play and choose a puzzle from the strip below the board.
  2. Tap a car to slide it one space in its allowed direction. Drag further to slide it several spaces at once.
  3. Use the arrow keys after focusing a car with Tab if you prefer keyboard control.
  4. Clear the third row and slide the red car to the right-hand edge. It drives out automatically.
  5. Finish within par slides for a perfect solve, or use undo and reset freely while you learn the layout.

Controls in detail

Tap-to-slide is the default on touch devices, and dragging a car moves it as far as your finger travels. The board scales to fit the screen, so all six rows stay visible in portrait.

Solving tips

  1. Count the exit row first

    Look at row three from the red car to the exit. Every car in that line has to move, and each one needs somewhere to go.

  2. Find the car with no options

    A car that cannot move in either direction is usually the one holding the whole puzzle together. Free it first.

  3. Work backwards

    Start at the exit and trace the path the red car needs. The last blocker is often easier to identify than the first.

  4. Do not shuffle for the sake of it

    Every move that does not create space for the red car is probably wasted. Par rewards intention.

  5. Use undo as a notebook

    Test a move, see what opens, then undo. You lose nothing and learn the layout faster.

Answers

Parking Jam questions

How many puzzles are in Parking Jam?

Eight. They start with a single blocker and finish with a full grid that needs a carefully ordered sequence.

Is there a time limit?

No. The only score is the number of slides you use. You can think for as long as you like.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes. Press Tab to move focus between cars, then use the arrow keys to slide the focused car along its axis.

What happens when I run out of moves?

Nothing — there is no move limit. If you get stuck, use undo to step back or reset to start the puzzle again.

Is Parking Jam the same as other games with that name?

No. This is an original GamesPlayLand puzzle with its own layouts. Other games with similar names are made by different developers.
